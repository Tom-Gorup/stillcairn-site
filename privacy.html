INFORMATION / PRIVACY

Privacy policy.

Stillcairn is a personal iPhone app developed by Thomas Gorup and is being prepared for public release. This policy describes the current app and this separate website. Effective October 10, 2026. A CybrPulse subdomain provides the pages and routed contact addresses; it does not establish an Apple App Store seller.

App storage

Stillcairn does not require an account and has no ads, analytics SDK, tracking, or developer-operated cloud backend. Profiles and Screen Time selection tokens, paired-card digests, schedules, completed session records, and local diagnostics stay in storage on the iPhone. The app shares scheduled profile selections with its on-device schedule monitor through an App Group so it can apply scheduled restrictions. A Home Screen widget reads a limited status snapshot through that App Group; the widget does not receive the card value or Screen Time selection tokens. The app does not send these records to a developer-operated service.

Screen Time, parent controls, and NFC

Stillcairn requests Apple's Family Controls authorization when you choose apps, categories, or supported websites to restrict during focus. iOS manages that permission and applies supported restrictions. Optional parent controls can request child authorization through Apple's Family Sharing flow on a child's iPhone. A parent PIN is not kept as readable text: the app stores a salted verifier in that device's Keychain and keeps local retry and lockout state. The app does not provide remote parent management. Family Controls availability and behavior depend on Apple's permissions and the device setup.

For a compatible NFC card, the app writes a random value and keeps a SHA-256 digest locally for matching. The raw card value is not included in diagnostics. A passive card can be copied by someone able to read it; Stillcairn does not claim clone resistance.

Insights and recovery

Insights calculate time in recorded Stillcairn sessions. They do not measure your actual use of other apps or estimate time saved. A confirmed lost-key recovery clears Stillcairn's restrictions and paired keys on that phone, then requires a fresh card write and verification. It does not erase a lost card or revoke a pairing on another phone. In parent mode, parent access is required for the appropriate recovery route.

Retention and iPhone services

Local records remain until you remove them or delete the app and its data. Offloading an app can retain data. Depending on iPhone settings, Apple's device backups may include local app data; backups and system diagnostics are managed by Apple under its own policies. Stillcairn does not add its own cloud backup or diagnostic upload.

This website and email

This static website has no form, marketing cookies, or analytics script. GitHub Pages and the DNS provider may process ordinary connection information, including IP addresses and request logs, to deliver it. If you email support or privacy, email routing and mailbox providers handle the address and message you choose to send so Thomas can reply. Please do not send NFC card contents, PINs, or private Screen Time selections.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, email privacy@cybrpulse.com. For product help, email support@cybrpulse.com. This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change.
