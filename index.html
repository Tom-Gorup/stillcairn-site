04 / THE DETAILS

Good questions.
Clear answers.

What kind of card do I need?+

A writable NTAG213, NTAG215, or NTAG216 NFC card. Choose a card with a roughly 25–30 mm antenna for easier scanning. For a metal surface, use an on-metal or ferrite-backed tag.

Can I pair more than one card?+

Yes. FocusKey supports up to 16 cards on one iPhone. You can name each key and remove an individual key while no focus session or scheduled window is active.

Can I use one card on another iPhone?+

Yes. Scan an existing FocusKey card in the second iPhone’s Add key flow. Each phone stores and manages its own pairing; removing a card from one phone does not remove it from the other.

What happens if I lose every card?+

Use the app’s confirmed Lost your key recovery. It clears FocusKey’s restrictions on that phone, revokes its paired cards, and requires a new card write and verification before another focus session. It cannot erase a lost physical card.

Does this lock my whole phone?+

No. FocusKey uses iOS Screen Time controls for the apps, categories, and supported websites selected in a profile. It does not change your passcode or lock the device. You retain control of Screen Time authorization in iOS Settings.

Is FocusKey available to buy today?+

FocusKey is in private iPhone testing. Pricing and public availability have not been announced. Email us to ask about early access and updates.
